Meta / Instagram Compliance

User Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: October 2026

In accordance with Meta Platform policies and international privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA), HaptixUI provides clear instructions for users who wish to request the deletion of any data associated with their Instagram interactions with HaptixUI Bot.

How HaptixUI Processes Your Data

HaptixUI Bot operates entirely stateless in volatile server memory. We do not store, maintain, or catalog persistent user profiles, passwords, contact details, or private chats in any permanent database. Comment IDs received via Meta Webhooks are held temporarily in short-lived memory queues only to prevent duplicate replies, and are automatically purged within 24 hours.

Steps to Request Data Deletion

If you wish to have any temporary logs or interaction records immediately wiped from our server memory, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to sachinmandawi@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - HaptixUI".
  2. Include your Instagram username handle in the message body.
  3. Our team will immediately verify and wipe any associated records within 24 to 48 hours and send you a confirmation.

Removing App Access via Instagram

You can also revoke permissions directly from your Instagram settings at any time:

  • Open Instagram > Settings > Website permissions > Apps and Websites.
  • Locate HaptixUI Bot and tap Remove.

For any privacy inquiries or immediate support, contact us at sachinmandawi@gmail.com.
